About us

Your technical partner for safer and more efficient environments

Kanaf is a Saudi company specialized in integrated security and technical solutions, and in delivering infrastructure for companies and their branches.

  • Surveillance: CCTV cameras and access control.

  • Networks, internal internet and telecom infrastructure.
  • Technical support, maintenance contracts and project management.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Kanaf supply and install CCTV cameras in Makkah?

Yes. We specialize in supplying and installing IP and analog CCTV cameras, DVR/NVR recorders, and maintenance for all types of facilities in Makkah and the holy sites. Every project starts with a site survey, then a solution design, programming and support.

Which security systems do you implement?

Surveillance systems, access control, intercom, fire alarm and smoke detection, and safety systems for facilities and sites.

Do you implement networks and technical infrastructure?

Yes. We implement wired and wireless networks, internal internet and telecom infrastructure, and prepare the networks that surveillance systems depend on.

Do you offer maintenance contracts for security systems?

Yes. We provide maintenance and technical support contracts for security, technical and infrastructure systems to keep operations continuous.

Do you carry out projects outside Makkah?

Yes. We have implementation experience in Mina, Muzdalifah and Arafat, and across several regions, with an integrated scope that supports operations across multiple sites.
