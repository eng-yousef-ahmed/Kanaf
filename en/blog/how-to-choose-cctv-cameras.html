How to Choose the Right CCTV Cameras for Your Facility

Before asking about price, know which camera type suits your site, where it should be mounted, and how many days you need to keep recordings.

Buying GuideOctober 11, 20262 min read

Choosing CCTV cameras does not start with the device itself. It starts with understanding the place you want to protect. The right camera for a shop entrance is not necessarily the right one for a parking lot or a large warehouse.

Start with the purpose of monitoring

Do you need monitoring as a deterrent only? To document events? Or to follow daily operations? Each purpose determines the camera type, its angle, and where it goes.

  • Entrances and exits: you need clear coverage of faces and vehicle plates.
  • Point of sale and cash areas: you need high detail and an angle that shows what happens.
  • Corridors and parking: you need wide coverage with fewer cameras.
  • Warehouses: you need good night vision and coverage of aisles between shelves.

Resolution and lighting

Higher resolution means finer detail, but it also increases recording size and storage needs. Do not choose the highest resolution for every point; choose it according to distance and purpose. Lighting matters too: a good camera in very low light may not show anything useful without night vision or supplementary lighting.

💡

Tip: before buying, ask for a site survey. A survey reveals blocked angles, real lighting, and distances. It is more accurate than any written specification.

Camera type and recording

IP cameras suit facilities that need flexibility for expansion and remote viewing. Analog cameras are economical for small projects. Read our article comparing IP and analog cameras for the details.

Questions to ask your supplier

  • What angle and distance does each camera cover on my site?
  • How many days will the system keep recordings with the proposed setup?
  • Can I add cameras later without replacing the main recorder?
  • Who maintains the system after installation, and what is the warranty period?
