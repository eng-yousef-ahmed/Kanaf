About this service

After installation, systems need someone to monitor them and solve problems quickly. We provide technical support covering faults, questions, and changes.

Our goal is to keep your business running without interruption due to a technical fault.

What is included?

  • Receiving and tracking support requests.
  • Resolving faults remotely or on site, depending on the case.
  • Changing settings and adding users and permissions.
  • Training staff on how to use the systems.
  • Periodic reports on system status.
