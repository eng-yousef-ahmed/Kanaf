Access Control & Intercom

Programming and implementation of access control and intercom systems, to manage people movement and keep operations safe and smooth.

WhatsApp us Call now Request a quote

About this service

An access control system decides who can enter which area and when, and records every entry and exit. We program the system according to the permissions of each user group.

Intercom systems let visitors be identified before a door is opened, and can be integrated with the control and surveillance systems to work together.

What is included?

  • Identifying entry and exit points and the right control method.
  • Supply of electric gates, locks, and card or fingerprint readers as required.
  • Supply and installation of intercom devices.
  • Programming of permissions, time schedules, and access reports.
  • Integration with surveillance where needed.
