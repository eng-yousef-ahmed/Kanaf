About this service

Every security or technical system depends on a stable network. We design the network according to the number of users and devices, and prepare the infrastructure that connects buildings and departments.

We work on both wired and wireless networks, and prepare surveillance networks so they run fast and steadily without interruptions.

What is included?

  • Requirements study and network design, including connection points.
  • Cable routing and installation of distribution panels and switches.
  • Installation of Wi-Fi access points and coverage planning.
  • A dedicated network for surveillance and control systems.
  • Network configuration and speed and stability testing.
